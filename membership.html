From $24/month - protect your home before issues become emergencies.

Why a Home Protection Plan Makes Sense

Most homeowners only think about plumbing, electrical, or HVAC service when something has already broken — usually at the worst possible time. A membership flips that around: regular checkups catch small problems (a corroding fitting, a failing capacitor, a breaker running hot) while they're still cheap and easy to fix, instead of after they've turned into a weekend emergency call. Here's what that's actually worth to you:

✓

Fewer Emergencies

Scheduled checkups catch wear and corrosion before they become a burst pipe or a dead AC on the hottest day of the year.

✓

Skip the Line

Members get priority scheduling, which matters most during heat waves, cold snaps, and holidays — exactly when non-members are waiting the longest.

✓

Real Savings Over Time

Member discounts apply automatically to repairs, and preventing one major failure typically covers a year or more of membership cost by itself.

✓

Longer Equipment Life

Water heaters, HVAC systems, and electrical panels last longer when they're serviced on schedule instead of run until something fails.

✓

One Team, Three Trades

Plumbing, electrical, and HVAC under a single membership means one call and one trusted team instead of juggling three different contractors.

✓

Predictable Budgeting

A fixed monthly cost is a lot easier to plan around than an unpredictable repair bill that shows up with no warning.

How Membership Works

  1. Pick the plan that fits your home. Not sure which one? Call us and we'll help you decide based on your home's age and systems.
  2. We schedule your maintenance visits. Depending on your plan, that's annual, bi-annual, or quarterly — timed ahead of the seasons that are hardest on your systems.
  3. We check plumbing, electrical, and HVAC systems for early warning signs — corrosion, worn parts, inefficient operation — and tell you honestly what we find, with no pressure to buy anything you don't need.
  4. When you need a repair, you go to the front of the line. Members get priority scheduling over non-members, plus their plan's discount applied automatically.
  5. Cancel anytime. Membership is billed monthly with no long-term contract — stay because it's useful, not because you're locked in.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a home protection plan actually cover?

Your membership covers the scheduled maintenance visits listed in your plan — system checkups, seasonal tune-ups, and priority scheduling. It is not a repair warranty: when something breaks and needs fixing, members pay for that repair, but at a discounted rate and with priority over non-member scheduling.

Can I cancel my membership anytime?

Yes. Memberships are billed monthly with no long-term contract. Call or email us to cancel, and you won't be billed for the following month.

Do I still pay for parts and materials during a maintenance visit?

Routine maintenance labor is included in your plan. If a visit uncovers a part that needs replacing — a worn valve, a failing capacitor, and similar — that part is quoted separately and your member discount applies automatically.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time to match your home's needs — just call us or mention it at your next visit.

Is membership worth it if my systems are newer?

Newer equipment still benefits from scheduled maintenance, which is often required to keep manufacturer warranties valid, and priority scheduling matters just as much whether your system is new or old when something unexpected comes up.
